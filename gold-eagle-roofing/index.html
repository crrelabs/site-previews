About Gold Eagle Roofing

Our dedication to honoring our commitments sets us apart. When we say we’ll be there, we mean it. Our team ensures clear communication, timely service, and a professional approach from start to finish. We pride ourselves on being reliable, allowing you to have peace of mind knowing your roofing needs are in capable hands. Our recent project in Orange City, FL covered over 500,000 square feet of shingle re-roof, 80,000 square feet of commercial low slope Torch down, 20,000 square feet of commercial low slope TPO, and over 50,000 square feet of commercial metal. This project included the re-roof of multi-family residential homes, hospital, assisted care facilities, and apartments. Given the scale and sensitive nature of the retirement facility, our focus was on executing the job effectively while maintaining normalcy for the residents. Organization, safety, and cleanliness were our top priorities throughout the project and were the main reason to our successful completion.
