About E2 Homes

We believe in using our talents and resources to help strengthen and grow those around us, and we’re committed to partnering with area organizations and institutions to give back. Notably, we served as the general contractor for the Emeril Lagasse Foundation Kitchen House and Culinary Garden. This unique space provides a hands-on, garden-to-table experience that helps to educate students and the community about healthy eating and sustainable living. Additionally, E2 has partnered with Habitat for Humanity of Seminole County & Apopka for the past several years, participating in several Home Builders Blitz events, and we’ve donated management and installation services to improve an area school’s playground. We’re also proud supporters of the Africa Windmill Project, Jewish Family Services (JFS) , Neighbors Network and REBUILD Globally.
