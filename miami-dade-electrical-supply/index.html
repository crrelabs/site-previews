About Miami Dade Electrical Supply

Miami-Dade Electrical Supply (MDES) was established in 1998 in Hialeah, Florida, a suburb of the greater Miami area. Since then, the company has grown to service the Southeast region of the State of Florida with 2 locations. Our company’s philosophy has been to provide our customers with a wide variety of products, at a competitive price, 7 DAYS A WEEK. Yes, our company is open 7 days a week. We are not talking about being available on the internet; no… we are so committed to servicing our customers that a few of us wake up on Sundays to go to work from 8 am-2 pm (EST). And if you are an early riser in our local market, then you will find us at the stores by 7 am. That’s not all, for most of the year, except during the winter, we are open Monday-Friday 7 am-6 pm, Saturdays 7 am-4 pm, and Sundays 8 am-2 pm; hours of operation that were unheard of prior to MDES entering the scene in 1998.
